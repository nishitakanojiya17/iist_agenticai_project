Why VidyaVault

Everything a Modern College Library Needs

Designed specifically for IIST — no more lost files, no more waiting in line.

🔍
AI-Powered Search
Type a question in plain English. The system understands meaning — not just keywords — and returns the most relevant documents instantly.
📦
Automatic Versioning
Every uploaded document creates a new version automatically. Old versions are never lost and can be restored with a single click.
🧾
OCR for Scanned PDFs
Tesseract OCR automatically extracts text from scanned documents, making even old photocopied papers fully searchable.
🔐
Role-Based Access
Students, faculty, and admins each see a tailored interface with only the tools and permissions appropriate for their role.
📊
Analytics Dashboard
Admins get a real-time view of search activity, document usage, user registrations, and a full tamper-proof audit log.
📱
QR Code Sharing
Generate a scannable QR code for any document — perfect for notice boards, kiosks, and quick sharing without logging in.
The Workflow

How a Student Finds a Document

Six steps, under three seconds, no staff assistance required.

1
Type Your Question
Ask in plain English — "Show me the 2024 CS question paper"
2
AI Vectorises Query
SentenceTransformers converts your words into semantic vectors
3
FAISS Searches Library
The vector database finds the most similar document chunks in milliseconds
4
Chunks Retrieved
The most relevant passages are pulled from matching documents
5
Llama 3 Writes Answer
A local AI model (Ollama) reads the chunks and generates a cited answer
6
Preview & Download
You see the answer with links to preview, download, or print the full document
