Why VidyaVault

Everything a Modern
College Library Needs

Designed specifically for IIST — no more lost files, no more waiting in line.

🔍
AI-Powered Search
Type a question in plain English. The system understands meaning — not just keywords — and returns the most relevant documents instantly using RAG + FAISS.
📦
Automatic Versioning
Every uploaded document creates a new version automatically. Old versions are never lost and can be restored with a single click.
📋
OCR for Scanned PDFs
Tesseract OCR automatically extracts text from scanned documents, making even old photocopied papers fully searchable by the AI.
🔒
Role-Based Access
Students, faculty and admins each see only what they need. JWT authentication with full RBAC — zero unauthorized access incidents.
📊
Analytics Dashboard
Real-time KPI dashboard, search funnel analytics, most-accessed documents, and one-click PDF & Excel export for administrators.
📱
QR Code Sharing
Generate instant QR codes for any document — share in classrooms, notice boards, or WhatsApp for zero-friction access.
The Workflow

How a Student Finds a Document

Six steps, under three seconds, no staff assistance required.

1
Type Your Question
Ask in plain English — "Show me the 2024 CS question paper"
2
AI Vectorises Query
SentenceTransformers converts your words into semantic vectors
3
FAISS Searches Library
The vector database finds the most similar document chunks in milliseconds
4
Chunks Retrieved
The most relevant passages are pulled from matching documents
5
Llama 3 Writes Answer
A local AI model (Ollama) reads the chunks and generates a cited answer
6
Preview & Download
You see the answer with links to preview, download, or print the full document
